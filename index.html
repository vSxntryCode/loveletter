Dear Alicia,

♥

This is not here because I think you need fixing. It is here because I see you.

I see how much you carry. I see you being a mum even when you are exhausted, sick, overwhelmed, or when everything seems to happen at once. I see how much of yourself you give to the people you love, even on the days where you barely have anything left for yourself.

And I need you to know something: I am so proud of you. Not because you never struggle. Not because you always hold it together. I am proud of you because you keep showing up, because you care so deeply, and because there is so much strength in you even when you do not feel strong at all.

You do not have to pretend you are okay around me. You can cry. You can rant. You can be quiet. You can tell me you have had enough for the day. You can just sit on the phone with me and say nothing. I never want you to feel like you have to make yourself easier for me to love.

I want to be the man who makes your world feel a little lighter. I want to cook for you, take things off your plate, make you laugh when your head is full, hold you when words are not enough, and be someone you know is going to show up. I cannot fix every hard thing for you, but I can make sure you do not have to face every hard moment alone.

You spend so much time looking after everyone else. You deserve to be looked after too.

And on the days where you cannot see how well you are doing, I will remind you until you can.

Love, Enzo ❤️
