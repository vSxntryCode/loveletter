Alicia,

Right. I'm going to be embarrassingly soft for a minute. You can laugh at me afterwards.

I still remember our first date at Hamilton Gardens. The flowers, getting to be around you properly, then us somehow ending up at Taco Bell. I'd go back to that day in a heartbeat. Not because it was some fancy, perfect date — because it was you.

You have so many little habits that I don't think you realise I notice. The tongue-out face on calls. How shy you get when I compliment you. The tiny hugs. The way you pull me back in for more cuddles. The play-fighting, despite the rather obvious size difference between us. Even your little snores. Yes, I said it.

I love you when you're all dressed up, when you're being silly, when you're wearing your glasses and making faces at me. Pretty much all the versions.

And something else I want you to know: I admire how much you care about the people you love. I see it in the little things. It makes me proud to be the person who gets to call you my girl.

I love you, gorgeous. And I hope when you look at this ridiculously over-engineered website, you can tell exactly how much.
