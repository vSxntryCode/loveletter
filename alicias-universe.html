A NOTE FROM ME · FOR YOU ONLY

Alicia,

I could have sent you a message, but I wanted to make you something you could actually explore. Something a little bit ridiculous, a little bit beautiful, and completely yours.

I love being with you. I love the excitement before I get to see you, and the way the time goes too fast when we're together. I love our hugs, the way we laugh over absolutely nothing, and how happy I am just having you beside me.

Sometimes I'll be doing something completely ordinary and I'll catch myself imagining you there. Cooking in the same kitchen. Going for a drive without much of a plan. Falling asleep next to you after a long day. Coming home and seeing you. Those are the kinds of things I find myself wanting.

There are so many places I want to take you and so many things I want us to do. But the part I'm most excited about isn't any particular trip or date. It's getting to share it with you.

I want to keep learning how to love you well. I want to be the one who makes plans and actually follows through with them. I want us to keep finding silly things to laugh at and reasons to stay a little longer together.

I don't know exactly what every year ahead of us looks like. I just know I really want you in it. I want more mornings, more evenings, more stories to tell, and more moments where I get to look over at you and think, there you are.

And if you ever wonder what all this is supposed to mean, it's very simple. I love you, Alicia. More than a webpage can say.

Love, Enzo ♡
